What Is Two-Factor Authentication?

Technology · 4 min read

Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds an extra layer of security to your accounts beyond just a password.

How it works

After you enter your password, the service asks for a second piece of information. This is usually:

  • A code sent to your phone by SMS
  • A code from an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, etc.)
  • A physical security key

Why it matters

Even if someone steals or guesses your password, they still cannot log in without the second factor. This blocks most common account takeovers.

Best practice

Use an authenticator app instead of SMS when possible. Authenticator apps are more secure than text messages.

Enable 2FA on your email, banking, social media and any important accounts. It is one of the simplest and strongest security steps you can take.
