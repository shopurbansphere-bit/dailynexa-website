How to Start Meditation

Health · 6 min read

Meditation does not require special equipment or long hours. You can start today with just five minutes.

1. Choose a quiet place

Find a spot where you will not be disturbed for a few minutes. It can be a corner of your room, a balcony, or even your bed.

2. Sit comfortably

Sit on a chair or on the floor with your back reasonably straight. You do not need a perfect lotus position. Comfort is more important than form.

3. Focus on your breath

Close your eyes gently. Notice the feeling of air entering and leaving your nose. When your mind wanders (and it will), simply bring your attention back to the breath without judgment.

4. Start small

Begin with 3–5 minutes. Use a timer so you are not checking the clock. After a week you can slowly increase the time.

5. Be consistent

Meditating for five minutes every day is better than one long session once a week. Try to practice at the same time each day.

That is all you need to begin. The benefits grow with regular practice.
