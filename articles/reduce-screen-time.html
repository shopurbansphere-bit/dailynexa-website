How to Reduce Screen Time

Sarah Jenkins
Lifestyle • 5 min read • ↓ SCROLL DOWN FOR KEY

Too much screen time can affect sleep, focus and mood. Here are realistic ways to reduce it without going extreme.

1. Track your current usage

Most phones have a built-in screen time report. Check it for a few days so you know where your time actually goes.

2. Remove tempting apps from the home screen

Move social media and video apps into a folder on the last page. The small extra effort reduces mindless opening.

3. Set app limits

Use your phone’s Digital Wellbeing or Screen Time settings to put daily limits on the apps that consume the most time.

4. Create phone-free zones

Keep the phone out of the bedroom, or at least away from the bed. Also try phone-free meals.

5. Replace the habit

When you feel the urge to scroll, have a simple alternative ready: a book, a short walk, stretching, or just looking out the window for a minute.

You do not need to eliminate screens completely. The goal is more intentional use and better balance.
