How to Improve Sleep

Health · 5 min read

Good sleep improves mood, memory, focus and overall health. These practical tips can help you sleep better starting tonight.

1. Keep a consistent schedule

Go to bed and wake up at roughly the same time every day, even on weekends. This helps set your body’s internal clock.

2. Reduce screen time before bed

Blue light from phones and laptops can delay sleep. Try to stop using screens 30–60 minutes before bedtime.

3. Make your room dark and cool

A dark, quiet and slightly cool room is ideal for sleep. Use curtains or an eye mask if needed.

4. Avoid caffeine late in the day

Coffee, tea and energy drinks can stay in your system for hours. Try to avoid them after mid-afternoon.

5. Create a simple wind-down routine

Reading a book, light stretching or a few minutes of deep breathing can signal to your body that it is time to rest.

Small changes practiced consistently often bring the biggest improvements in sleep quality.
