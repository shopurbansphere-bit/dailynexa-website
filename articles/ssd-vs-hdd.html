SSD vs HDD

Technology · 5 min read

When buying a laptop or computer, you often choose between SSD and HDD. Here is a simple comparison.

HDD (Hard Disk Drive)

HDDs use spinning magnetic disks. They are cheaper per gigabyte and still good for storing large amounts of data such as movies and backups. They are slower and more sensitive to physical shock.

SSD (Solid State Drive)

SSDs have no moving parts. They are much faster, quieter, use less power, and are more resistant to drops. The main downside is higher price per gigabyte.

Which should you choose?

  • For the operating system and programs → SSD is strongly recommended. The computer will feel much faster.
  • For large media storage → HDD can still be useful and more economical.
  • Best of both → Many people use a small SSD for the system and a larger HDD for files.

If your budget allows only one drive, choose an SSD. The speed difference is very noticeable in daily use.
